A little about me

I'm a backend-leaning full-stack engineer who has spent 6+ years turning ambiguous requirements into systems that hold up in production — designing the schema, building the API on top of it, and shipping the interface that calls it. I've architected a dental EMR and a manufacturing ERP from scratch, rebuilt a meditation platform's backend off a legacy CodeIgniter API, and spent two years owning a school ERP's marks and events systems before any of that. Laravel, Flutter, and most of my API instincts were all picked up on the job — I learn new stacks fast, and work just as well solo as embedded in a team. Formally trained in biomedical engineering, which mostly taught me to be suspicious of systems that look like they're working.
